## About This App

This application allows you to create unique QR codes by embedding images into the gaps between the squares of a QR code.

A visually appealing QR code can catch people's attention and encourage them to take out their smartphones, open their cameras, and scan it.

The embedded image also makes your QR code more recognizable, helping people identify suspicious replacements or fake QR codes.

The application is built with JavaScript and runs entirely in your browser, so your data is never sent to an external server.

I designed this application to be simple, intuitive, and easy for anyone to use.
